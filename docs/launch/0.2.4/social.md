## LinkedIn

> Most "AI follow-up" tools work the same way: a bot joins your call, the recording goes to their cloud, and you get a summary you still have to turn into an email.
>
> Sentwise for Mac takes the other route. When your call ends, you give it the transcript you already have: pasted, a .txt, .md, .vtt or .srt file, or a folder it watches, such as Zoom's local recordings. It drafts the next-steps email in the voice it learned from your Sent mail: a recap, action items with an owner for each, and a proposed next meeting.
>
> You read the full draft in Review Drafts and approve it. Approving saves it to your Gmail Drafts or sends it, your choice. Nothing goes out without that step.
>
> Stored mail, your voice profile and your transcripts stay on your Mac. Only the text for each request passes through our stateless proxy, and we do not log or retain request or response bodies. Managed drafts do not train models.
